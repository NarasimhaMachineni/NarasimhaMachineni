## `01` — About Me

I am a **Computer Science Engineer** focused on building data-driven and intelligent solutions at the intersection of **clinical programming, clinical data, software engineering, AI and healthcare technology**.

My primary technical focus is **R, R Shiny, Python and clinical data standards such as SDTM and ADaM**, with additional experience in SAS programming, TLF development, statistical programming and dashboard engineering.

I enjoy transforming complex datasets and technical requirements into **clean analytical workflows, interactive dashboards, clinical reporting solutions and practical AI-powered applications**.

My goal is to build technology that is not only technically strong, but also **useful, maintainable, scalable and aligned with real-world healthcare and clinical requirements**.

### Primary Expertise

- **R Programming** — data manipulation, analytical programming, visualization and clinical-data workflows.
- **R Shiny** — interactive dashboards, reactive applications and clinical analytics interfaces.
- **Python** — data processing, automation, analytics, AI/ML and application development.
- **Clinical Data** — structured clinical datasets, data transformation, validation and analytical workflows.
- **SDTM** — clinical data standardization concepts, domains and structured clinical datasets.
- **ADaM** — analysis datasets, analysis-ready data concepts and clinical analysis workflows.
- **TLF Development** — Tables, Listings and Figures concepts and clinical reporting workflows.
- **SAS Programming** — intermediate-level SAS programming for clinical and analytical workflows.
- **AI & Healthcare Technology** — intelligent, data-driven applications for healthcare environments.
- **AI Product Engineering** — converting AI capabilities into practical, user-focused products.
